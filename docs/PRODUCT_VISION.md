# Product Vision — Personal Economic Agency & Collective Opportunity Intelligence

## Mission
Make individual people stronger from Day One. Give each person a private AI representative that helps protect their interests, recover value, discover opportunities, access resources, build income and enterprises. As participation grows, consent-controlled collective intelligence should unlock opportunities no individual could efficiently access alone.

## Positioning
**Intelligence for the individual. Power for everyone.**

This is not merely a budgeting app, cashback marketplace, ad-funded finance app or conversational financial assistant. Finance is the entry point into a broader system of individual agency and collective economic coordination.

## Day-One Personal Mode
A person must receive meaningful utility even when they are the only user and no marketplace network exists. A Personal Power Discovery flow gathers only information the person chooses to provide or connect and returns a clear report with a concrete first action.

- Money recovery: identify possible duplicate or incorrect charges, refunds, subscriptions, warranty and compensation opportunities; prepare evidence and claims.
- Protection: explain suspicious payment requests, contract terms, unexpected recurring charges and consumer risks, with evidence and confidence labels.
- Benefits: discover scholarships, public schemes, subsidies, concessions, insurance reimbursements, grants and employer benefits. A match is a lead, not proof of entitlement; official confirmation is required.
- Opportunity engine: match skills, availability, experience and constraints with legitimate work, projects, training and income pathways.
- Capability graph: turn demonstrable knowledge into teaching, consulting, micro-project and service opportunities.
- Personal enterprise: assist with demand discovery, business modelling, pricing, storefront/catalogue creation, customer enquiries, orders, invoices and profit tracking.
- Crisis shield: help in income disruption or emergencies through cashflow replanning, legitimate relief discovery, obligation prioritization and income opportunities. Do not default to recommending new debt.

## Eight Opportunity Pillars
1. **Personal AI Representative / Guardian:** work for the user's interests; detect potential errors; assemble evidence; draft refund, warranty, compensation and consumer complaints; track progress and escalation where supported.
2. **Opportunity Engine:** connect skills, time, knowledge and goals with jobs, freelance work, projects, training and income opportunities; support applications and follow-through.
3. **Entitlement Engine:** match against authoritative rules for scholarships, subsidies, benefits, grants, concessions, reimbursements and employer benefits; distinguish estimated eligibility from confirmed award.
4. **Resource Exchange:** match underused human and commercial capacity with verified demand—tutor hours, surplus produce, inventory, equipment, storage, transport, seats, spaces and services.
5. **Crisis Shield:** identify pressure, prioritize essentials, find legitimate relief, locate new income paths and coordinate voluntary support with permission and human oversight.
6. **Knowledge-to-Economic-Asset:** create a user-controlled capability graph and turn proven knowledge into paid or reciprocal services, teaching and consulting.
7. **Economic Defence / MyBudget Shield:** assess suspicious messages, payment instructions, anomalous charges, hidden fees and contract risks. Show the evidence and uncertainty; never claim fraud certainty without proof.
8. **One-Person Enterprise Engine:** help users validate local demand, plan costs, create a catalogue/storefront, acquire customers and operate business workflows through authorized tools.

## Killer Move — Collective Economic Intelligence Network / Commons
Personal agents may, only with explicit purpose-specific consent, coordinate shared demand, discover unused resources and connect people with suppliers, institutions, services, capacity, funding and opportunities.

- Aggregate demand without exposing individual household records to providers or other users.
- Discover group purchasing and collective bargaining opportunities.
- Match local supply, skills, inventory, services, capacity and logistics to demand.
- Let authorized agents compare offers and negotiate within explicit user constraints.
- Require user approval for consequential commitments and transactions.
- Make discounts, commissions, sponsor contributions and user shares transparent.
- Support voluntary mutual aid and crisis coordination without exposing sensitive personal details.

The network must not be required for first-day usefulness. Personal Mode works independently; Commons Mode adds network effects as consented participants and verified partners join.

## Financial Foundations
- Household income, expenses, commitments, debt obligations and recurring payments.
- Cashflow projections, budget allocation and goal planning.
- Offer comparison and net-value optimization, including conditions and extra spend.
- Evidence-backed value ledger: money recovered, verified savings, earnings received, benefits received, fees avoided, and additional costs.
- Proposed revenue share: 50/50 of contractually defined eligible net partner revenue, not gross ad impressions or uncollected amounts. Direct discounts and earmarked support must be passed through as agreed.
- No sale of identifiable household financial histories. Research participation is a separate opt-in, purpose-bound process subject to privacy law and appropriate safeguards.

## Architecture Direction
- **Personal Agent:** private user context, goals, capability graph, authorizations and outcome history.
- **GENIEA:** agent orchestration, mission planning, tool execution and bounded multi-agent coordination.
- **KRN:** personal and collective opportunity knowledge graphs, with strict separation between private and explicitly shared edges.
- **MAKOS:** operational workspace and durable workflow execution layer.
- **Meta AI models:** language, multimodal understanding, planning and agentic reasoning where appropriate.
- **Deterministic services:** money arithmetic, eligibility rules, constraints, offer ranking, authorization and verified-value accounting.
- **Infrastructure:** transactional persistence, idempotent commands, event outbox, durable jobs, audit records, observability, data minimization and consent enforcement.

These are long-term architectural directions. They are not all implemented in Iteration 00.

## North-Star Metric
**Verified Individual Value Created (VIVC)** = verified money recovered + verified expenditure savings + additional verified earnings + benefits actually received + demonstrably prevented fees/penalties − additional costs incurred.

Do not count estimated opportunities as delivered value. Track time to outcome, successful completion rate, false-positive rate, evidence quality, user control and privacy incidents alongside VIVC.

## Demo Narrative
The demo must explain today's working system and the full final vision without confusing the two. Use one coherent scenario:
1. One person joins; Personal Power Discovery produces a useful insight and a first action without any Commons users.
2. User-provided records feed deterministic cashflow and obligation analysis.
3. Guardian identifies a sample billing discrepancy, links it to evidence and drafts a user-approved dispute.
4. Opportunity and entitlement engines surface a tailored possibility and state what remains unverified.
5. A few opted-in users contribute only a narrow shared demand signal; Commons matches an eligible provider/resource.
6. The offer evaluator rejects a misleading offer whose extra spend or conditions erase net benefit.
7. A transparent ledger separates realized value, estimates, provider funding, platform fees and the user's share.
8. An unexpected expense triggers replanning and a user approval checkpoint.
9. The final vision expands to a human economic opportunity network connecting people, businesses, institutions, resources and funding.

Any non-live partners, simulated agents, synthetic users, projected revenue or hypothetical benefits must be labelled in the demo. Never imply a payment, claim, award or merchant offer occurred if it did not.

## Brand Direction

The final product name is not selected. `MyEconomy` is the repository and working project name only. The user wants a lively, modern, memorable and globally pronounceable name and has ruled out Sanskrit-derived and Christian/religiously coded naming directions. Do not assume Arabic-rooted naming is mandatory or treat any previously suggested name as approved. Complete collision, pronunciation, domain, app-store and trademark screening before final adoption.
